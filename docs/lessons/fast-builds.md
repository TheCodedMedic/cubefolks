# Builds must be fast (the user, 2026-10-28: "The build is too slow")

The Ep11 first minute took 1.5 h to build on 10 sessions. The time went to waiting and rework, not building:
~35 min waiting for the shared set (builders used stand-ins, then rebuilt), ~40 min of three-way rounds to fix one contact
(the handrail hit: positions, train speed and impact time were not in the brief), ~20 min of coordinator idle time
(timed check-ins instead of merging on each report), and heavy self-review.

**How to apply (every episode):**
- **Shared sets FIRST:** build the shared sets (and the shared effect) BEFORE the scene builders start, ideally while the
  owner reviews the previous gate. Scene builders never start against placeholders.
- **Decide every contact and timing in the brief:** positions, speeds, contact times, facing, the exact spot a character
  stands on, anchored to words. No builder-to-builder negotiation.
- **More, smaller windows:** about 15 builders of 30–40 s each for the rest of a film.
- **Tight builder budget:** a first pass in 20 min; one review sheet; frame-by-frame runs only through motion; ONE speed test.
- **Merge on each report**, immediately; review as they land. Never wait on a 25–40 min timer.
- **Warm the render sessions** during the final review.
- Target: the rest of a 10-min film built in ~40–50 min, rendered in ~15–20 min on 20 sessions, finished in ~10.
