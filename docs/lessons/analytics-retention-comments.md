# What the first YouTube numbers taught us (2026-11-11)

Clever Hans ("The Smartest Horse Who Ever Lived?") and the rat bounty Short, about 8 hours in: ~1,500-1,700 views each, ~75% stayed
instead of swiping, ~80% average viewed (52-54 s of ~65 s). Good hooks and pacing. Two problems:

1. **Viewers leave once the secret is out.** In Clever Hans the reveal lands at 45-50 s, then ~17 s of wrap-up followed; watch time
   fell right there. Rules from now on:
   - put the "You see..." reveal late, ending at about 80-85% of the runtime;
   - after the reveal: one punchy payoff line and the loop line, **8 seconds at most**.
2. **Zero comments.** The story resolved everything and gave no reason to talk. Rules from now on:
   - an **on-screen question pop-in** in the last 3-4 seconds, while the narration runs into the loop (never spoken: a spoken question
     breaks the loop), e.g. "Did YOU guess the trick? 👇";
   - a **pinned comment** for every upload, written with the title and description: a light, open question viewers can answer from
     their own life ("What's the weirdest thing you've ever microwaved?").
3. **The hook must be crazy** (the owner, 2026-11-11): the first line is the most extreme claim in the story, stated flat as
   fact, under about 2 seconds to the strange part. (More hook guidance from the owner to follow.)
4. **Don't open with the title or a year** (the owner, 2026-11-11): most of our Shorts opened "In 19XX..." and it became repetitive.
   Line 1 is the strange thing itself; the year is a pop-in. Vary the opening shape from Short to Short.
5. **No slow first 1.5 seconds** (vidIQ pre-publish forecast on the "11 days awake" Short, 2026-11-11, shown by the owner): "the first
   1.5 s are spent on a slow introduction of the '11 days awake' text; cut them so it starts immediately with the text and the character's
   reaction." Rule: on **frame 0** the hook pop-in is already fully on screen (no slow build, no fade or type-on in; at most a 3-4 frame
   pop), the main character is already mid-reaction, and the camera is already moving. No establishing beat, no settle. The narration
   starts at the lead (0.05 s). Check frames 0, 15 and 45 before rendering: frame 0 alone must tell the hook.
