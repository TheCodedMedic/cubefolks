The user, 2026-10-23, after Episode 04 v3 and again planning Part 2: "Ensure you don't make the background sounds too loud. They are not the main thing, except in places where not many words are being said and it is tense action… in calm scenarios, you don't want to make it too loud." Also: "I don't want a lot of different music playing… a kind of background sound that is just playing, not always changing."

**Why:** the voice and story are the main thing. In places Ep04's beds and music fought the narration, and constant cue changes felt busy.

**Update (2026-10-23, Part 2 review):** music was still too high. Go about 4–6 dB lower (calm music about −26 dB under speech). Also: one track for the whole film is wrong. **Different scenes need different songs**: each major scene or place gets its own fitting track, but don't switch every 30 s.

**How to apply:**
- **Calm and talky scenes:** music about −26 dB RMS under speech (v1's −21 was still too high). Ambience beds about −32.
- **Tense action with few words** (races, slow motion, the hook): music may come up to about −16 under speech and louder in gaps.
- **A different song for each major scene or place** (superseded the earlier 'one theme' rule on 2026-10-23), with crossfades at scene changes; still no new track every 30 seconds.
- Measure the music-only mix against the voice before finishing.

**Update (2026-10-24, Ep06):** a sound must end when its source leaves: long library recordings (train rumbles, horns)
go in as beds with an end time and a fade, never as one-shot effects that play out their full length.

**Loops posing as one-shots (2026-10-25):** several library effects are long loops (footsteps_tile 24 s, heartbeat 10–40 s,
tick 23 s). Played as "one step" or "one beat" they ran on for half a minute (the user heard footsteps from the knock to
Dani's shop). `mix.py` now cuts every one-shot to an optional 4th element `keep_s` (else `SFX_MAX`, 8 s) with a short
fade; single steps are cut into the episode's `sfx_gen/step1–4.wav`. Check an effect's length before cueing it.

**Update (the user, 2026-10-28, Ep11 first minute): "not enough sound effects and background sounds, too low as well."**
The −24/−26 calm-music levels and the builders' effect levels were now too quiet. New defaults: calm music about −20 dB
under speech (−19 to −21), the title track about −10 (loud, not ducked), builder effects +5 dB over what they write,
ambience beds +6 dB (about −20 to −26). Every scene gets a full ambience layer (a busy platform, a carriage rumble, wind
over the city, a low drone under science), a whoosh on every scene change, and a riser into the spoken title.
