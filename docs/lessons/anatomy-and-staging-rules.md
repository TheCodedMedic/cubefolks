From the user's Part 2 review (2026-10-23); the full list is in `episodes/05_go_back_in_time_part2/fix_audit_v1.md`.

**Bodies and contact:**
- Nothing passes through anything: hands through tables, heads merging (a hug looking like a kiss), bodies into a bus.
- Every seated pose keeps the forearms on the tabletop or in the lap.

**Movement:**
- Eating looks real: a pinch grip on the spoon, the spoon stops in front of the mouth, the mouth opens.
- Walking and running always move the legs. Never slide a character.
- Runners pump arms and legs hard, Flash-like.
- Animals get real gaits.

**Action:**
- If something hits someone, show the hit before the rewind.

**Staging and camera:**
- Show both characters clearly in two-person action shots, and give the older you a different lightning colour.
- Hugs: heads side by side, staged from a side or three-quarter view. Show stills for approval first.

**Infographics:**
- Always include humans: drawn scientists, participants and our hero reacting. Never data alone.

**Effects and transitions:**
- Use lots of effects and camera moves.
- A white flash on every timeline or time jump.
- Disintegration is gradual (it evaporates from the edges), not a few particles.
- Text-only screens get small background animations.

**Sets:**
- A named place must read clearly (the river shows water).
- Doors and rooms are fully designed, and a door opens into a visible room.
