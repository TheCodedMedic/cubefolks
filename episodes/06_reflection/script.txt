In my town, if your reflection blinks before you do, you have three days left.

Nobody knows where those people go. Their beds are made, and their shoes are still by the door.

So every morning, my whole family stands at the bathroom mirror and blinks together.

Last week, it was Jenny from next door. She told me at recess, and she laughed, like it was a joke.

On day three, her bike was still on the lawn, and her house was dark.

After that, I started noticing little things.

My dad started shaving in the kitchen, with no mirror at all.

My mom covered every mirror in the house with towels. She said it was for the dust.

And at dinner, my little brother wouldn't look at his spoon.

Tonight, I pulled the towel off the bathroom mirror.

All three of them came to the doorway behind me.

But in the mirror, I was standing there alone.

They were never blinking with me. They were watching me blink.

And this morning, for the first time, my reflection blinked first.
