A truck with no speaker sings outside my window at 3 a.m. every night.

Mom says we never take its free ice cream.

Toby took one last week.

The next day, he held his cone all through lunch without taking a bite.

It didn't melt, even on the hot slide.

When he walked past me, I heard the truck's song.

There wasn't a truck anywhere.

A week later, my teacher had a cone too.

During our spelling test, she hummed the tune while she wrote.

She even hit the same wrong note the truck always hits.

Last night, the truck stopped outside our house.

The engine went quiet, but the song kept getting closer.

Mom opened my bedroom door holding two cones.

I backed away.

The music followed her.

Then she opened her mouth.

The whole truck song came out, bells and all.

She held the spare cone out to me.

I was supposed to be its next speaker.
