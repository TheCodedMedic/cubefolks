Our house blinks at our neighbor every night to keep him away.

Dad always rushes home before nine, even from the middle of the grocery store.

At nine, Mr. Bell blinks his porch light three times and watches our window.

Dad flashes ours back.

I thought they were planning something.

Then Dad hurt his knee and fell asleep on the couch.

Nine o'clock came, and our porch stayed dark.

Mr. Bell flashed again.

I waved from behind the curtain, but he couldn't see me.

He stood there in his slippers, staring at our window.

I didn't know whether to wake Dad or pretend nobody was home.

Then he grabbed his coat and hurried across the street.

I opened the door before he could knock.

He wasn't angry about the light.

He was scared something had happened to Dad.

Their secret code meant someone would always come.

Tonight, I took Dad's turn at the switch.

Mr. Bell went home knowing we were safe inside.
